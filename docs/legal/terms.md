## 1. The service

Aira Career Copilot is a hosted service that helps an Organization's Candidates find jobs, prepare applications and, where the Organization allows it, submit them. The Organization pays for the service and decides how it is used for its Candidates, within the plan it has chosen. These terms describe that relationship; the [privacy notice](privacy.md) describes how personal data is handled, and the [data-processing agreement](dpa.md) governs personal data the Organization instructs us to process.

## 2. Accounts and roles

1. The first person to sign up for an Organization becomes its owner and must confirm their email address. The owner confirms they have authority to bind the Organization.
2. The Organization decides who its members are and their roles (owner, admin, coach, candidate, billing contact, auditor). It is responsible for what its members do with the service and for keeping their access current. Removing a member ends their sessions.
3. Each person must keep their credentials secret. Owners and admins on plans that include single sign-on must use multi-factor authentication.
4. The service is for organizations. An individual outside an Organization is not offered an account in this release.

## 3. Plans, trials and payment

1. Plans, limits and prices are in [pricing.md](../pricing.md) and on the pricing page `[URL]`. Limits are enforced by the service, not only displayed.
2. A trial lasts 14 days and has the limits of the trial plan. If no plan is chosen by the end, the Organization's data becomes read-only and can be exported for 30 days, after which it is deleted.
3. Charges are per candidate seat per month `[currency, taxes]`, billed through our payment provider. An upgrade applies immediately with a prorated charge. A downgrade or seat reduction applies at the end of the billing period; if the Organization is then over the new plan's limits, automation pauses until it is within them.
4. If a payment fails, automation stops at once and the owner and billing contact are notified. After 14 days unpaid the Organization becomes read-only. After 60 days a deletion date is set 30 days later and a final notice is sent. Paying restores access if the data has not been deleted.
5. Cancelling stops renewal; service continues to the end of the period, followed by a 30-day export window and then deletion.
6. Enterprise terms, limits and invoicing are set in a separate order form `[reference]`.
7. Usage above a plan's limit stops at the limit on Starter. On plans that allow metered overage, the order form or plan states the per-unit price.

## 4. Acceptable use

The Organization and its members must not: use the service for anything unlawful or deceptive; apply for jobs for someone who has not agreed to it; enter another person's credentials; attempt to bypass limits, authentication or isolation between organizations; probe, scan or load-test the service without written permission; use the service to harass recruiters or employers or to flood employer systems; or ask the service to solve CAPTCHAs, guess answers to application questions, or sign in to an employer's site on a candidate's behalf. The rules the automation itself follows are in [automation-and-linkedin.md](automation-and-linkedin.md).

## 5. Automatic applications

1. Automatic submission is off unless the Organization's policy and its plan allow it **and** the Candidate (or a coach the Organization has given approval rights) has turned it on in the Candidate's own search plan. Otherwise the service fills the form and waits for a person to submit.
2. The service never invents answers. It answers only from information the Candidate has entered and confirmed. Sensitive information (work authorization, visa, demographics, salary) is entered and confirmed by the Candidate only, and a question that needs it is held for the Candidate.
3. Every submission stores evidence (a screenshot and the confirmation text) and an audit entry naming who or what acted and for which Candidate. The Candidate can see what was done in their name.
4. The Organization is responsible for ensuring that its use of automated applications is lawful in the markets it serves, including any duty to provide human review. `[Market-specific language to be added after legal review.]` The Organization can require review mode for all Candidates or by region.
5. Employers' application systems change without notice. We do not promise that an application will succeed, only that failures are held or reported rather than guessed.

## 6. LinkedIn

LinkedIn's User Agreement forbids automated access. Using LinkedIn features of the service (searching, reading, Easy Apply, inbox replies) goes through the Candidate's own LinkedIn session and may lead LinkedIn to restrict that account. These features are off by default, are enabled per Candidate only after the Candidate has read and acknowledged the notice in [automation-and-linkedin.md](automation-and-linkedin.md), can be turned off by the Candidate or the Organization at any time, and can be withdrawn by us. `[Position to be confirmed by counsel.]`

## 7. Content and data

1. The Organization and its Candidates own their data (profiles, resumes, answers, messages, documents). We process it only to provide the service and as the Organization instructs, as the data-processing agreement describes.
2. We use job-board data under each board's terms (attribution, links back, no republication, polite caching).
3. When a Candidate leaves an Organization, the Organization's setting decides whether the Candidate receives an export of their data and the workspace is deleted (the default), or the Organization keeps the workspace archived.
4. We keep a billing record and an audit stub for legal retention after an Organization is deleted, and nothing else about it.

Our operators do not see a customer's data unless the Organization's owner approves a time-limited support-access request (at most 24 hours); the Organization sees a banner while access is active and the access is logged. Support levels depend on the plan.
