# Family

A private place for your family to stay close: share news and photos, chat live, plan get-togethers and never
miss a birthday. Everyone in the household has their own profile (on a shared device, tap your name; in the
hosted product you are your account), so Family knows who wrote what.

Open it from the apps menu (or `g` then `7`). Today shows new messages, the next family events and this week's
birthdays.

Family is a section of the Social app (`/social`): its tabs are folders in the left menu, **+ New** offers
**Family event**, and the search box finds the people in your household and your family events (see
[Social: In the Aira frame](social.md#in-the-aira-frame)).

## Feed

* Write something, add photo links (https, up to six) and, if it is a big moment, mark it as a milestone
  (birthday, new job, holiday, new baby, wedding, graduation, new home, proud moment).
* React with ❤️ 👍 😂 😮 🎉 (tap again to take it back) and comment.
* Adults can pin a post to the top and remove any post or comment; everyone can edit and remove their own.
* The feed updates by itself while it is open; the menu shows how many posts you have not seen.

## Chat

* **Family chat** is one room for everyone. Start a one-to-one chat with anyone in the family from the list.
* A one-to-one chat is private: nobody else in the household can open it.
* Messages arrive live (every few seconds). Under your last message you see who has read it ("Seen by Ana").
* Press Enter to send (Shift and Enter for a new line), or use the big Send button. You can delete your own
  messages.
* A new private message also pops up as a reminder in Aira (and can be read aloud in easy mode).

## Calendar

* Add an event: what, when (or all day), where, notes, and who is invited (nobody ticked means everyone).
* Everyone invited answers **Going**, **Maybe** or **Can't go**; the event shows who is coming.
* Events of the next seven days are on Today; an hour before an event you are going to, Aira reminds you.
* **Add to my phone's calendar** downloads the events you are invited to as a calendar file (.ics) that a phone,
  Outlook or Google Calendar imports.
* The person who added an event, or an adult, can change or cancel it.

## People and dates

* Everyone in the household, with a button to message them.
* Birthdays from the household's profiles, plus relatives' birthdays and anniversaries you add (with or without
  the year). They are listed soonest first, with how old someone turns.
* **Send wishes** posts a birthday or anniversary message to the feed. A birthday shows **Gift ideas** (here and on Today) when the
  person has a wish list.
* A child can remove a relative's date they added themselves; adults can remove any.

## Wish lists

* Everyone keeps a list of gift ideas: what, where to find it (an https link) and a note (size, colour).
  Grown-ups also look after a child's list.
* **I'll get this** tells the rest of the family you are buying it, so nobody buys the same present twice. The
  person it is for never sees who is getting what, or whether anything is taken: the surprise stays a surprise.
  Only the person who said it can take it back.
* The owner marks a wish **Got it** (received) or removes it.
* In the hosted product a list shows your own list, lists with wishes and children's lists you look after, and
  names the others who have not added anything yet.

## The family board (adults)

* A link for grandparents, aunts, uncles and friends who do not have an account. They see the family feed
  (never your chats), react, and write a message with their name.
* Their messages wait for an adult to approve them, unless you turn on "Publish messages from relatives at once".
* Turn on **Show our wish lists on the board** and relatives see the wish lists too (gift ideas not received yet)
  and tap **I'll get this** with their name. The board never shows which gifts are taken (the person a gift is
  for may open it too); a relative who picks one someone already took is told so. In the app the family sees
  "Granny said on the family board they are getting this", and any grown-up (not the person it is for) can tap
  **Free it up again** if it was a mistake.
* Turn the link off at any time; a new link can be made later. Children cannot create or manage the board.

## Privacy

* One-to-one chats are only for the two people in them; the family chat, feed and calendar are for the
  household (an event can be limited to the people invited).
* The board shows only the feed's names, emoji and posts; never chats, email addresses or settings. It is
  headed with the family, not the account's business (no business name, logo or contact details).
* In the hosted product the family is your organization's members; each member sees only their own private
  chats.
