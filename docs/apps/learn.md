# Learn

A little learning every day, for every age: children practising times tables, adults learning a language,
grandparents brushing up on staying safe online. Each person in the household has their own progress. Adults can
also earn from Learn by selling the decks they make.

## Studying

* **Decks** are sets of flashcards (a front, a back, an optional hint and example). A deck is yours, or shared with
  the household: everyone studies a shared deck with their own progress, and only its maker can change it. Anyone
  who may study a shared deck can **Make my own copy** to change it freely (the copy starts with fresh progress).
  A deck someone else is selling cannot be copied (it is still studied as it is), and a copy of another person's
  deck cannot be put on sale.
* **Study** shows one card at a time. Think of the answer, press **Show answer** (or Space), then say how it went:
  **Again** (1), **Hard** (2), **Good** (3) or **Easy** (4). Cards come back just before you would forget them
  (spaced repetition, the SM-2 method): a card you knew comes back tomorrow, then in about 6 days, then further
  apart; a card you missed comes back in the same session.
* **Practise my hardest cards** (on Study, or **Practise these now** under Progress) gives you the cards you have
  missed before and do not know well yet (coming back less than three weeks apart), hardest first, whether or not
  they are due. Practising a card early is extra practice: a right answer does not move the day it comes back (only
  a miss does: it comes back today), and it counts toward XP and the goal once per card a day. A card answered right
  today waits until tomorrow; when all are done the tab says so. A card answered again on the same day earns
  nothing more anywhere.
* Each day has limits: new cards (10, 8 for children) and reviews (100). Change them under **Today > Change goal**.

## Finding, adding and downloading cards

* **Search and filters** on Decks: type to find a deck by its title or description (starter decks too), and show
  all decks, the ones you made, the ones shared with you, the ones with cards due now, or the ones on sale.
  A deck with more than 8 cards has a **Search cards** box in its drawer.
* **Add many cards**: paste a list, one card per line, the front and back split by `=`, a dash with spaces
  (` - `), a comma, a semicolon or a tab. The one most lines use is read first; a line without it is read with the
  one it has, so a list may mix them. A spreadsheet's rows or a CSV file with the columns front, back, hint, example
  work too (quoted cells may hold commas, quotes and several lines), and a first line "front, back" is skipped. Cards already in the deck
  (the same front) are skipped, so pasting a list twice adds nothing; lines without a front and a back are listed
  with their line number. At most 500 lines at a time, and 1,000 cards in a deck.
* **Download CSV** in a deck's drawer gives its cards (front, back, hint, example) to keep, print or move to another
  flashcard app; the file reads back in through Add many cards unchanged.

## Progress

The **Progress** tab shows your own numbers:

* **The last 12 weeks**: a square a day, darker the more you practised (cards studied, plus reading minutes);
  ✓ marks a day the goal was met and ❄ a day kept by a streak freeze. Each square says its day and numbers to a
  screen reader.
* **You remember**: the share of reviews in the last 30 days you did not answer Again (shown after 5 reviews; extra
  practice of cards that were not due does not count).
* Your streak and best streak, cards and minutes this week, cards studied in all, days practised and cards known
  well (coming back three weeks or more apart).
* **Coming up**: how many cards come back for review today and on each of the next 7 days.
* **Your hardest cards**: the ten you missed most and do not know well yet, with **Practise these now**.
* **Download my learning log (CSV)**: one line a day (cards, new cards, minutes studied, reading minutes, XP, goal met,
  streak freeze).

## Goals, streaks and rewards

* **Daily goal**: a number of cards (15, 10 for children) or minutes. Reading can count toward it (2 minutes of
  reading = 1 card for a cards goal).
* **Streak**: days in a row the goal was met. Miss one day and meet the goal the next: this week's **streak freeze**
  keeps the streak (one a week). In the evening (after 18:00 in the business's time zone) Aira reminds you when a
  streak would break.
* **XP and levels**: 5 XP for a card you knew, 2 for one you missed, 2 more for a new card, 20 for meeting the goal,
  5 for each right quiz answer (10 more for a perfect quiz), 3 for each right maths answer, and 1 a minute of reading
  (up to 30 a day). Level 2 starts at 50 XP, level 3 at 200, level 4 at 450.
* **Badges**: first deck, first card, 3, 7 and 30 days in a row, 100 and 1,000 cards, a perfect quiz, 25 maths
  answers, 5 hours of reading, level 5.

## Decks to start with

Starter decks are added in one click (your own copy): times tables (2 to 6, 7 to 12), spelling for children, world
capitals, everyday Spanish and French, healthy living, staying safe online, first aid basics and the solar system.

**Make a deck on any topic**: type a topic, who it is for and how many cards. The writing model writes the cards
(check them). Without a model, Aira uses a matching starter deck when one fits, or a set of questions that guide you
to learn the topic yourself, marked "Written from a template" so you can edit the answers.

## Quizzes and maths

* **Quizzes** are multiple choice, made from a deck (the wrong options are other cards' answers). Answers are checked
  on the server and shown after you hand in. With no deck to quiz yet, the tab points you to Decks and to maths.
* **Maths** practice by level: adding and taking away to 20, times tables, dividing, adding fractions, percentages.
  Relaxed has no clock; timed gives a little time for each question (more for children and seniors), and late
  answers are marked but earn no XP. Fractions can be typed as 3/4, 1 1/4 or 0.75.

## Reading and the family

* **Reading**: add books, log minutes (10, 20, 30 or any; the book you are reading now is picked for you), mark a
  book finished or **Reading it again**, change its title or author, or delete it (its minutes go with it).
* **Family**: this week's XP, streaks, cards and levels for everyone in the household (names and numbers only).

## Sharing a deck

An adult can share a deck they made as a page anyone can study in their browser without signing in (nothing they do
is saved). Turn the link off at any time, except while the deck is on sale (its page is where buyers pay and study:
stop selling first). Children cannot share decks outside the household.

## Earn: selling a deck

Adults can earn from the decks they make (children cannot sell, and do not see the Earn tab).

1. Open a deck you made (at least two cards) and choose **Sell this deck**: a price, how many cards people may try
   for free first (1 to 20; at least one card is always kept for buyers) and **how buyers pay you** (your payment
   link, or instructions such as bank details). A deck is never put on sale without a way to pay. Its public page is
   made for you, and stays on while the deck is on sale. A deck on sale keeps at least two cards.
   **Stop selling** makes the page free again and closes the requests still waiting for payment; people who bought
   it keep their access.
2. Share the link. Visitors study the free cards, see the price and who made it, and ask to buy with their name and
   email. They get a reference (like `LN-7KQ2XD`) and your payment link or instructions, right under the form. Aira
   does not take card payments. The same email asking again gets the same request back. A form error shows next to
   the form (in view on a phone) and goes away on the next try.
3. **Earn** shows how buyers pay you (**Change** it there) and lists the requests (search by buyer, email, reference
   or deck; filter by status; **Download CSV** follows the search and the filter). When the money has arrived, press
   **Payment received**: the access code is shown to you, with **Email the buyer** (an email with the code and the
   page's link, ready to send from your own mail).
4. The buyer enters the code on the same page (**Already bought it?**) to study every card; their browser remembers
   it. **Send a new code** replaces a lost one (the old code stops working). **Not paid** takes a sale out of your
   earnings and turns its code off; paid again later, it counts on the new payment date. **Cancel** closes a request
   that will not be paid. Deleting a deck closes its open requests.

Today shows a **Deck sales** card, which opens Earn, when people are waiting for a payment to be confirmed.

**Whose money it is.** On your own computer the account is the household's: a seller may leave the payment fields
empty to use the payment link in the business profile (Business hub > Business profile), sales join the Business
hub's revenue, and the access-code email also goes through the outbox (approve it there, or turn on automatic
sending for `deck_access`). In the hosted build a member's deck income is their own, as for a Stocks analyst: they
set their own payment link (the organization's is never used), and nothing goes into the organization's revenue,
activity feed or outbox (which members cannot open, and where admins could read the code). Each member sees only the
requests for their own decks.

Learn's hub summary (cards studied this week, earned from decks this month, deck sales to confirm, decks) reaches the
assistant's context; the Business hub's home lists only the business apps, so it does not show these cards there.
Deck revenue still counts in the hub's revenue totals on the local build.

## In the Aira frame

Learn lives at `/learn`, with its own logo, the search box across the top, **+ New** above the menu and the launcher,
like the other Aira apps. The page is named after the open tab (*Today*, *Decks*, *Reading*...), with a line about it.

* **+ New** offers *New deck*, *Make a deck on any topic*, *Add cards to a deck*, *Add a book* and *Log reading
  time*; each opens that tab with its form open (the address drops `?new=` straight away, so Back and refresh do not
  open it again). *Add cards* goes straight to the card form when you have made one deck, asks which deck when you
  have several, and offers *New deck* when you have none; the deck then opens at the new card. Every profile, a
  child's too, has its own decks and books, so everyone is offered all five (selling stays under Earn, for adults).
* **Search** suggests up to six of your decks (by title, description or who shared it), cards (either side, the hint
  or the example; cards whose question matches come first) and books (title or author), each opening itself: a deck's
  drawer, the deck's drawer at that card (marked for a moment), or the book on Reading. **Enter** shows everything that
  matches on Decks: the matching decks, then *Cards that match* and *Books that match*, with the words in the page's
  own search box and a link back to every deck.
* **The menu's folders** are Learn's tabs: Today, Study, Decks, Progress, Quizzes, Maths, Reading, Family and (for
  adults) Earn, each with its own icon. On a wide screen they replace the tab bar under the title; on a phone, where
  the menu is a sheet, the bar stays and each tab keeps its own create buttons.

A starter deck you have already added shows *added* and opens your copy instead of making another one, and the
Family tab says how to add the people you live with when you are the only one on it.

## For developers

`aira/suite/learn/` (`store.py` tables `ln_*` including `ln_sales`, `service.py` rules, `progress.py` statistics and
the weak-card queue, `cards_io.py` import and CSV, `sales.py` selling, `starters.py` starter decks, `learn_routes.py`
routes under `/api/learn`, Today card, pulse, hub checklist and summary, and the public `deck` page with its `buy`
action and `cards` query; the access code travels in the `Aira-Secret` header and is stored hashed),
`views-learn.js`, `public-learn.js`, `app-learn.css`, tests in `tests/test_suite_learn.py`,
`tests/test_suite_learn_features.py`, `tests/test_suite_learn_fixes.py`, `tests/test_suite_learn_ui.py`,
`tests/test_suite_learn_search.py` and `tests/test_suite_learn_shell_ui.py` (the frame).
`ln_sellers` holds each seller's own payment link and instructions; `ln_decks.copied_from` the first owner of a
copy; `ln_reviews.practice` marks an answer given before the card was due.

Routes added for these features: `GET /api/learn/stats` (`?format=csv` for the learning log), `GET /api/learn/study?mode=weak`,
`POST /api/learn/decks/{id}/import`, `GET /api/learn/decks/{id}/export`, `POST /api/learn/decks/{id}/copy`,
`POST /api/learn/decks/{id}/price`, `GET /api/learn/sales` (`status`, `q`, `format=csv`),
`POST /api/learn/sales/{id}/paid|unpaid|cancel` (`paid` answers the code and `message`, the email ready to send),
`POST /api/learn/seller` (`payment_link`, `payment_instructions`; the price route takes them too), and `PATCH /api/learn/books/{id}` now also takes `title` and `author`.
`GET /api/learn/search` (`q`, `limit` of each kind, at most 50) answers the search box: `{decks, cards, books}` holding every
word of `q`, from the decks this person may study and their own books.
