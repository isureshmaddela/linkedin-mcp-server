# Social

Aira's own social network and messenger: a profile, friends and followers, a feed with photos and stories,
private and group chats, groups, pages for businesses and a work profile, all in one simple app that is safe for
every age. Unlike your household's Family space, Social connects you with people outside your home: everyone on
this computer (every Aira account) or, in the hosted product, members of every organization that has Social.

Open it from the apps menu. The menu badge counts your unread messages and notifications; a new message also
pops up as a reminder (read aloud in easy mode). Today shows **From your friends**: chats with new messages and
friend requests, with an **Accept** button, and for a grown-up, a child's new friend waiting for a **Say yes**.

## Getting started

* The first time, choose your **@name** (Aira suggests one), your display name, a picture (an emoji or an
  https:// photo link) and a few words about you.
* Choose who can see your profile: **Everyone** or **Only my friends**. Change it any time under Profile.

## Friends

* Search by name or @name. **Add friend** sends a request; the other person accepts or declines it.
* **Follow** someone with a public profile to see their public posts without being friends.
* **People you may know**: friends of your friends and people from your household (or organization).
* **A grown-up says yes to a child's new friends.** When a child asks someone from outside their household to be
  friends (or accepts such a request), nothing is sent until a grown-up of the household says yes: under Friends,
  **Your children's new friends** shows who it is (an adult or young, friends in common) with **Say yes** and
  **Say no**, and Today shows the same. The child sees "Waiting for a grown-up to say yes" and gets a note with the
  answer. Friends from their own household need no yes.
* **Block** someone and they cannot see your profile or posts, find you, message you or send a request. You stop
  being friends. Blocking works both ways and across organizations. Unblock them under Friends.

## Feed

* Write a post (up to 2,000 characters) with up to six photo links, for **Everyone**, **Friends** or **Only me**.
* React with ❤️ 👍 😂 😮 😢 🎉 (tap again to take it back), comment, and share public posts.
* `#tags` link to every post with that tag you may see; `@name` mentions notify that person.
* Edit or delete your own posts and comments; the author of a post may delete comments on it.
* **Stories** last 24 hours, for your friends. You see who has seen yours.
* The feed updates by itself while it is open (new posts, comments and reactions). A comment you are typing keeps
  its text and place while the feed updates. "Edited" shows only when the author changed the post.

## Chats

* Message a friend, or anyone who chose "Let people who are not my friends message me" (adults only).
* Group chats: name the chat and tick your friends. The chat's admin adds and removes people; when the last
  admin leaves, the longest member takes over.
* Messages arrive live (every two seconds). Under your messages you see **Sent** or **Seen**. Scroll up to read
  older messages: the chat follows new ones only while you are at the bottom.
* The rules for who may message whom apply to old chats too: after someone unfriends a teen (or an adult stops
  accepting messages from people who are not friends), the old chat can be read but not written in.
* Send a photo link; delete a message for everyone within an hour of sending it.
* **Mute** a chat to stop its reminders and badge; **Hide** a one-to-one chat until the next message.
* On a phone the list and the conversation are separate screens; on a wide screen they are side by side.

## Groups and pages

* **Groups** are public (anyone joins) or private (people ask, an admin lets them in). Members post in the
  group; a private group's posts are only for its members.
* **Pages** are for shops, clubs and services (adults only). Follow a page to see its posts in your feed. A page
  can link to the business's own Aira public page (https).
* A group's or page's admins **change** it (name, about, who can see a group; category and link of a page) and
  **delete** it: it and its posts disappear for everyone, its authors included.

## Marketplace (buy, sell and earn)

Sell what you no longer need to friends and neighbours, and buy from them. Children do not see the Marketplace;
teens may look and message sellers who are their friends; adults sell.

* **Sell something**: what it is, the price (empty or 0 to give it away; something that is not a number, such as
  "ten", is refused), the currency, category, condition, where to pick it up, up to six photo links, who can see
  it (everyone on Social, or only friends) and your own payment link (optional; Aira never handles the money).
* A listing is **yours**: it shows only your payment link, its public page carries your name, and what you sell
  counts in your own "Earned from sales". People who run the account's business (on this computer the household's
  adults; hosted, the organization's owner, admins and coaches) also see **Sell it for <business>**: the listing
  then uses the business's payment link (if you leave yours empty) and a sale is added to the **Business hub**.
  Other members never sell with the organization's link or write into its books.
* **Browse**: search words, a category, the highest price, newest or cheapest first. **Save** a listing to find it
  again under Saved.
* **Message the seller**: the chat starts with "Is it still available?" and stays open between the two adults even
  when they are not friends, while the listing is for sale (and two weeks after it sold, to arrange the pick-up).
  Once it is removed, hidden or no longer for them, the chat follows the usual rules again (for example only
  friends, if that is your setting). The chat shows a link back to the listing.
* **Your listings**: what is for sale, reserved and sold, how many people looked and saved each, and **what
  selling has earned you** (a sold listing you remove still counts). Mark a listing reserved, sold (to whom and
  for how much: the listed price unless you change it; 0 if you gave it away) or available again. A sale for the
  business is added to the **Business hub** (revenue from the Social app; a giveaway adds nothing); "Available
  again" takes it back out.
* **Share it outside Aira** (a listing for everyone): a page anyone opens without an account with the photos,
  price, where to pick it up and a **Pay** button for the listing's payment link, headed with your name (or the
  business's, for a listing sold for the business). Visitors tap **I'm interested** and leave
  their name and how to reach them; you get a notification. Turn the link off at any time.
* Listings can be reported like posts; a moderator can hide one. Your listings are in your data download and are
  erased with your account.

## Profile and work

* Your profile shows your posts, friends and followers. A private profile shows strangers only your name.
* **Work profile**: a headline, open to work, skills and experience (one job per line: Title | Company | Start |
  End).
* Adults with a public profile can **share** it as a public page anyone can open without Aira: name, about,
  work and public posts. Turn the link off at any time.
* **Download my data** (Profile) saves everything you wrote or set on Social as a file: your profile, posts,
  comments, messages you wrote, stories, friends and listings (never other people's words).

## Safety for every age

* **Children** are private, cannot be found by strangers (only by friends and their own household), receive
  messages only from friends, never post for everyone, cannot share their profile, make groups or pages, and
  join only private groups an admin approves.
* **Teens** are private and receive messages only from friends.
* **Report** any post, comment, message, story, listing or person. Links and photos must be https; posts are plain text
  (no HTML). Words on the blocked list are replaced with stars. Posting, commenting, messaging, friend requests
  and reports are limited per hour.

## Moderation

* On this computer the household's owner sees **Moderation**: reports filed by their household or about their
  household's people. **Hide it** hides the post, comment, message or story for everyone; **Suspend the person**
  (only someone in their household) hides everything they wrote and stops them posting; **Nothing wrong**
  closes the report. The owner also edits the blocked words.
* In the hosted product moderation belongs to Aira's platform operators (an organization's owner cannot hide
  another organization's content): **Social moderation** in the platform console (support and platform admins,
  `platform.moderation.manage`) lists open, resolved and dismissed reports with a preview, hides, suspends or
  reinstates, dismisses, and edits the blocked words (masked with asterisks). Every action is in the platform
  audit log (`social.moderate`, `social.suspend`, `social.unsuspend`, `social.blocklist`).

## In the Aira frame

Social lives at `/social` with its own logo, the search box across the top, **+ New** above its menu and the
launcher, like the other apps (`auto_apply_assistant/shell.js`). Each page is named after its tab (**Feed**,
**Chats**, **Family calendar**), since the bar above already says Social.

* **+ New** offers **Post** (the feed's composer, focused), **Story** (the *Add a story* form), **Chat** (the *New
  chat* form with your friends), **Marketplace listing** (the *Sell something* form) and, when Family is open to
  you, **Family event** (the family calendar's *Add an event* form). Each asks with `?new=<kind>` and the page drops
  it from the address once the form is open, so Back or a refresh does not open it again. Children and teens get
  no Marketplace listing (they do not sell), the same as the pages hide **Sell something** for them.
* **Search** ("Search people, chats, groups and the Marketplace") suggests, a few of each kind in turn, people (by
  name or @name, with how you are related), your chats (by name or their last message), public groups, pages,
  things for sale (not for a child) and, from Family, people in your household (choosing one opens a chat with
  them) and family events (the calendar opens with the event highlighted). A `#tag` offers its posts. **Enter**
  opens **Results for "…"** (`#/social?tab=search&q=…`): everyone and everything that matches, by kind, with
  **Add friend** or **Message** beside each person; with no match it offers **Find people** and **Search the
  Marketplace**.
* **Folders**: Social's tabs (Feed, Chats, Friends, Groups, Pages, Marketplace, Profile, Notifications and, for
  the household's owner, Moderation) and Family's tabs are folders in the left menu, each with its own icon, and
  the unread chats and notifications are counted on them. Only the open section's folders show. On a wide screen
  the folders stand in for the page's own tab bar; a phone keeps the bar, since its menu is a sheet.

## Where the data lives

Social has one database for the whole server (`social.sqlite3`): next to the accounts database on this
computer, in the data folder of the hosted product. People are identified by their household profile (this
computer) or their organization membership (hosted), never by email address.

Because the file sits outside every organization's database, the hosted product handles it explicitly: a
person's own export carries `social.json` (their profile, posts, comments, messages they wrote, stories,
listings and friends, never other people's words); removing a member erases their Social account and everything they wrote;
deleting an organization erases all of its members' accounts. Groups, pages and chats they made stay for
the other people in them. Tests point Social at a scratch file (`tests/conftest.py`), never the real one.
