# Social

Aira's own social network and messenger: a profile, friends and followers, a feed with photos and stories,
private and group chats, groups, pages for businesses and a work profile, all in one simple app that is safe for
every age. Unlike your household's Family space, Social connects you with people outside your home: everyone on
this computer (every Aira account) or, in the hosted product, members of every organization that has Social.

Open it from the apps menu. The menu badge counts your unread messages and notifications; a new message also
pops up as a reminder (read aloud in easy mode). Today shows **From your friends**: chats with new messages and
friend requests, with an **Accept** button, and for a grown-up, a child's new friend waiting for a **Say yes**.

## Finding your way

* On a wide screen the left menu is Social's rail: **Home**, **Explore**, **Reels**, **Chats**, **Friends**,
  **Dating** (adults), **Groups**, **Pages**, **Marketplace**, **Notifications**, **Saved** and **Profile**, each with
  its icon and a count of what is new. On a phone a bar along the bottom holds the five used most (Home, Explore,
  Reels, Chats, Notifications) and **More** opens the rest (and Monosage's own menu).
* **+ Create** (top right on every page) makes a **post**, a **photo** post, a **story**, a **reel** (in the Videos
  app's uploader), a **poll**, a **group**, a **page** or a Marketplace **listing**. Children make no groups;
  children and teens make no pages or listings.
* Every list that is empty says what to do next (find friends, follow a page, share the first post...). Light and
  dark themes follow Monosage's; nothing scrolls sideways on a phone.

## Getting started

* The first time, choose your **@name** (Aira suggests one), your display name, a picture (an emoji or an
  https:// photo link; afterwards a photo from your device) and a few words about you.
* Choose who can see your profile: **Everyone** or **Only my friends**. Change it any time under Profile.

## Photos from your device

* Posts, stories, your profile picture and cover photo, group and page pictures and Marketplace listings take
  photos from your phone or computer (https links still work too). Up to six on a post or listing.
* The browser shrinks a big photo before sending it, and Monosage takes out its hidden details (where and when it
  was taken, the camera: EXIF, XMP, comments) before keeping it. JPEG, PNG and WebP, up to 8 MB.
* A photo is only shown to people who may see what it is on: it loads through an address made for them that lasts a
  few hours (in the hosted product too). A shared public page (your profile, a listing) shows only its own photos.
* Give each photo a short description for people who cannot see it. A photo you uploaded but never used is removed
  after a day; your photos are removed with your account.

## Friends

* Search by name or @name. **Add friend** sends a request; the other person accepts or declines it.
* **Follow** someone with a public profile to see their public posts without being friends.
* **People you may know**: friends of your friends and people from your household (or organization).
* **A grown-up says yes to a child's new friends.** When a child asks someone from outside their household to be
  friends (or accepts such a request), nothing is sent until a grown-up of the household says yes: under Friends,
  **Your children's new friends** shows who it is (an adult or young, friends in common) with **Say yes** and
  **Say no**, and Today shows the same. The child sees "Waiting for a grown-up to say yes" and gets a note with the
  answer. Friends from their own household need no yes.
* **Block** someone and they cannot see your profile or posts, find you, message you or send a request. You stop
  being friends. Blocking works both ways and across organizations. Unblock them under Friends.

## Home and posts

* Home has the stories row at the top, **On this day** (your own posts from earlier years on today's date, when
  there are some), the composer and the feed; on a wide screen a column beside it suggests how to finish your
  profile, people you may know and popular #tags.
* Write a post (up to 2,000 characters) with up to six photos, for **Everyone**, **Friends**, **Close friends** or
  **Only me**. Several photos show as a carousel you swipe (or move with the arrows); tap one to see it large.
* Add a **feeling or activity** (😊 feeling happy, ✈️ travelling...), a **place**, and a **filter** for the photos
  (Warm, Cool, Vivid, Black and white, Faded, Vintage, Dramatic: the photo is kept as sent, the filter is how it is
  shown).
* **Polls**: the post is the question, with two to four different answers, open for 1, 3 or 7 days. Everyone who
  may see the post votes once; the results show after you vote, when the poll is over, and always to its author.
  When it ends, the author and everyone who voted hear which answer won.
* React with ❤️ 👍 😂 😮 😢 🎉 (tap again to take it back), comment, and share public posts.
* **Replies**: answer a comment and the reply sits under it (one level: a reply to a reply joins the same thread).
  **Like** a comment. Removing a comment removes its replies.
* **Save** a post into a collection of your own (or All saved): see **Saved**.
* **Pin** up to three of your own posts to the top of your profile.
* `#tags` link to every post with that tag you may see; `@name` mentions notify that person.
* Edit or delete your own posts and comments (the ⋯ menu on a post); the author of a post may delete comments on it.
* The feed updates by itself while it is open (new posts, comments and reactions). A comment you are typing keeps
  its text and place while the feed updates. "Edited" shows only when the author changed the post.

## Close friends

* Under Friends, tap **Add to close friends** beside a friend (or ⭐ on their profile). Only you see the list.
* A post or story for **Close friends** reaches only the people on it who are still your friends; unfriending or
  blocking takes someone off.

## Stories

* **Stories** last 24 hours, for your friends (or only your close friends: their ring is green). The viewer shows
  one story after another with a bar for each; tap the sides or use the arrow keys, hold to pause.
* On a friend's story: **react** with one tap (❤️ 😂 😮 😢 👏 🔥) or **reply**: the reply goes to them as a private
  message in Chats, under the usual rules for who may message whom.
* On yours: **Seen by** lists who saw it and how they reacted; **Add to a highlight** keeps it.
* **Highlights** keep chosen stories on your profile after their day is over, under a name (Summer, Family...).
  They are for the same people as the stories in them.

## Explore

* **Popular now**: public posts of the last two weeks with the most reactions, comments, shares and votes, as a
  grid; popular **#tags**; **people to follow** (friends of friends, people from your household or organization, and
  public adults with many followers).
* **Search** people, posts (their words or place), groups, pages and #tags (`#gar` finds #garden).
* Everything follows the usual rules: only what you may see, never someone who blocked you or whom you blocked. A
  child sees only posts from their friends and their own household, and no strangers to follow.

## Reels

* Tall short videos from the **Videos** app, one after another: scroll, swipe or use the arrow keys; the one in view
  plays (sound off until you turn it on). **Like** and **comment** count in Videos; **Share** copies its link;
  **Create a reel** opens Videos' uploader. Without Videos (hosted, when the organization has not turned it on) the
  page says where reels come from.

## Saved

* Your **collections** (and All saved), each with its cover photo and how many posts. Make, rename and delete
  collections (a deleted collection's posts stay under All saved). A saved post that is removed, hidden or no longer
  for you drops out. Marketplace listings you saved stay under Marketplace › Saved.

## Notifications

* One list for reactions, comments, replies, likes on your comments, mentions, follows, friend requests, poll votes
  and results, story reactions and replies, Marketplace interest and a child's new friends, grouped by Today, This
  week and Earlier. Filter it (Unread, Mentions, Comments, Reactions, Follows and requests, Polls, Stories), open one
  (it is marked read) or **Mark all as read**.

## Chats

* Message a friend, or anyone who chose "Let people who are not my friends message me" (adults only).
* Group chats: name the chat and tick your friends. The chat's admin adds and removes people; when the last
  admin leaves, the longest member takes over.
* Messages arrive live (every two seconds). Under your messages you see **Sent** or **Seen**. Scroll up to read
  older messages: the chat follows new ones only while you are at the bottom.
* The rules for who may message whom apply to old chats too: after someone unfriends a teen (or an adult stops
  accepting messages from people who are not friends), the old chat can be read but not written in.
* Send a photo from your device (or a photo link); delete a message for everyone within an hour of sending it.
* **Mute** a chat to stop its reminders and badge; **Hide** a one-to-one chat until the next message.
* On a phone the list and the conversation are separate screens; on a wide screen they are side by side.

## Groups and pages

* **Groups** are public (anyone joins) or private (people ask, an admin lets them in). Members post in the
  group; a private group's posts are only for its members.
* **Pages** are for shops, clubs and services (adults only). Follow a page to see its posts in your feed. A page
  can link to the business's own Aira public page (https).
* A group's or page's admins **change** it (name, about, who can see a group; category and link of a page) and
  **delete** it: it and its posts disappear for everyone, its authors included.

## Marketplace (buy, sell and earn)

Sell what you no longer need to friends and neighbours, and buy from them. Children do not see the Marketplace;
teens may look and message sellers who are their friends; adults sell.

* **Sell something**: what it is, the price (empty or 0 to give it away; something that is not a number, such as
  "ten", is refused), the currency, category, condition, where to pick it up, up to six photo links, who can see
  it (everyone on Social, or only friends) and your own payment link (optional; Aira never handles the money).
* A listing is **yours**: it shows only your payment link, its public page carries your name, and what you sell
  counts in your own "Earned from sales". People who run the account's business (on this computer the household's
  adults; hosted, the organization's owner, admins and coaches) also see **Sell it for <business>**: the listing
  then uses the business's payment link (if you leave yours empty) and a sale is added to the **Business hub**.
  Other members never sell with the organization's link or write into its books.
* **Browse**: search words, a category, the highest price, newest or cheapest first. **Save** a listing to find it
  again under Saved.
* **Message the seller**: the chat starts with "Is it still available?" and stays open between the two adults even
  when they are not friends, while the listing is for sale (and two weeks after it sold, to arrange the pick-up).
  Once it is removed, hidden or no longer for them, the chat follows the usual rules again (for example only
  friends, if that is your setting). The chat shows a link back to the listing.
* **Your listings**: what is for sale, reserved and sold, how many people looked and saved each, and **what
  selling has earned you** (a sold listing you remove still counts). Mark a listing reserved, sold (to whom and
  for how much: the listed price unless you change it; 0 if you gave it away) or available again. A sale for the
  business is added to the **Business hub** (revenue from the Social app; a giveaway adds nothing); "Available
  again" takes it back out.
* **Share it outside Aira** (a listing for everyone): a page anyone opens without an account with the photos,
  price, where to pick it up and a **Pay** button for the listing's payment link, headed with your name (or the
  business's, for a listing sold for the business). Visitors tap **I'm interested** and leave
  their name and how to reach them; you get a notification. Turn the link off at any time.
* Listings can be reported like posts; a moderator can hide one. Your listings are in your data download and are
  erased with your account.

## Profile and work

* Your profile has a **cover photo** and your picture (tap the camera on either to change it from your device), your
  name, @name, a few words, **links** (https only, up to five, each with a label), and counts of posts, friends,
  followers and following: tap followers or following for the lists (people you may not see are left out).
* Under them: your **highlights**, then **Posts** (pinned first) | **Photos** (a grid) | **Reels** (from Videos) |
  **Tagged** (posts that mention you).
* **Finish your profile** shows how complete it is and the next steps (a picture, a cover, a bio, a link, a first
  post, three friends).
* A private profile shows strangers only your name.
* **Work profile**: a headline, open to work, skills and experience (one job per line: Title | Company | Start |
  End).
* Adults with a public profile can **share** it as a public page anyone can open without Aira: name, about,
  work and public posts. Turn the link off at any time.
* **Download my data** (Profile) saves everything you wrote or set on Social as a file: your profile, posts,
  comments, messages you wrote, stories, friends and listings (never other people's words).

## Dating

Meet new people, for adults only. Dating is off until you turn it on, and it has its own profile: nothing about
it appears on your Social profile, feed, chats list, badges or reminders.

* **Adults only.** Only someone who is an adult on their household profile (or hosted membership) and 18 or over
  by their birthday may open Dating. The birthday comes from the household profile when it has one; otherwise
  you enter it once and confirm you are 18 or over. Children and teens never see Dating, every Dating route
  refuses them, and nobody under 18 is ever shown to anyone. If a household profile later says teen, or a
  birthday under 18, that dating profile is hidden at once.
* **Set up in four steps**: about you (first name, birthday, gender, city or area, height if you like), photos
  (uploaded from your device or https links, up to six) and a few words, three prompts (chosen from a list of
  questions, with your answers), and who you'd like to meet (what you are looking for, show me women, men or
  everyone, the ages, up to ten interests). Matches see only this profile: your first name, age, area, photos and
  what you chose to share, never your @name, Social name or birthday.
* **Discover** shows one big card at a time: photos, name, age, area, prompts and interests, with the interests
  you share highlighted. **Like**, **Pass** or **Star** (a like that is shown first, marked "Starred you"), with
  the buttons, the arrow keys or by dragging the photo. You have 30 likes and 3 stars a day; passes are not
  limited and **Undo** takes back the last pass. People who starred you come first, then the most interests in
  common, the same area, the same hopes and who was here most recently. **Filters** change who you see and the
  ages. Both people's preferences must fit: you only see someone who would also see you.
* **Likes you** shows how many people like you and only a hint of each (a blurred initial and the interests you
  share). **Meet them** shows them one by one in Discover; liking back makes the match, and a pass is never told.
* **A match** ("You both said yes!") opens a private chat in Social's chat system that stays open while the match
  lasts, even when you are not friends and do not take messages from strangers. Only matches can message. The chat
  suggests icebreakers from their prompts and the interests you share. **Unmatch** deletes the chat for both and
  you never see each other again; nobody is told why.
* **Privacy**: **Hide me from my Social friends** (they don't see you, you don't see them). People of your own
  household never see each other here; hosted, people of your own organization don't either unless you turn
  **Hide me from my own organization** off. Blocked people (Social's blocks, both ways) never appear, and
  **Block** from Dating blocks them everywhere in Social and ends the match.
* **Report** a profile (and block in the same step): it goes to Social's moderators (Aira's platform operators,
  hosted) as a report about that person, starting with "Dating profile:". You stop seeing them. A suspended
  account disappears from Dating for everyone. On this computer the household's owner reviews reports, as for the
  rest of Social.
* **Pause** hides you from Discover and keeps your matches and chats. **Delete dating profile** removes the
  profile, every like and pass, every match and its chat; your Social profile stays. **Download my dating data**
  saves your dating profile, your likes and passes and the messages you wrote.
* **Safety tips** (in Dating's menu): chat here first, meet in a public place, never send money, trust your
  instincts, report what feels wrong.
* **Hosted**, Dating connects members of every organization that has Social, like the rest of Social. An
  organization's owner and admins have no view of a member's Dating: its routes act only for the person asking,
  they are never written to the organization's audit log (``HOSTED_RULES = "private"``), and an export of a member
  carries nothing of it. Removing a member or deleting an organization erases their Dating with their Social account.

## Safety for every age

* **Children** are private, cannot be found by strangers (only by friends and their own household), receive
  messages only from friends, never post for everyone, cannot share their profile, make groups or pages, and
  join only private groups an admin approves.
* **Teens** are private and receive messages only from friends.
* **Report** any post, comment, message, story, listing or person. Links and photos must be https; posts are plain text
  (no HTML). Words on the blocked list are replaced with stars. Posting, commenting, messaging, friend requests
  and reports are limited per hour.

## Moderation

* On this computer the household's owner sees **Moderation**: reports filed by their household or about their
  household's people. **Hide it** hides the post, comment, message or story for everyone; **Suspend the person**
  (only someone in their household) hides everything they wrote and stops them posting; **Nothing wrong**
  closes the report. The owner also edits the blocked words.
* In the hosted product moderation belongs to Aira's platform operators (an organization's owner cannot hide
  another organization's content): **Social moderation** in the platform console (support and platform admins,
  `platform.moderation.manage`) lists open, resolved and dismissed reports with a preview, hides, suspends or
  reinstates, dismisses, and edits the blocked words (masked with asterisks). Every action is in the platform
  audit log (`social.moderate`, `social.suspend`, `social.unsuspend`, `social.blocklist`).

## In the Aira frame

Social lives at `/social` with its own logo, the search box across the top, **+ New** above its menu and the
launcher, like the other apps (`auto_apply_assistant/shell.js`). Each page is named after its section (**Home**,
**Chats**, **Family calendar**), since the bar above already says Social. Home is still the `feed` tab, so old links
keep working.

* **+ New** offers **Post** (Home's composer, focused), **Photo** (the composer with the photo chooser), **Story**
  (the *Add a story* form), **Reel** (Videos' uploader), **Poll** (the composer with a poll), **Chat** (the *New chat*
  form with your friends), **Group**, **Page**, **Marketplace listing** (the *Sell something* form) and, when Family is
  open to you, **Family event** (the family calendar's *Add an event* form). Each asks with `?new=<kind>` and the page
  drops it from the address once the form is open, so Back or a refresh does not open it again. Children get no
  Group; children and teens get no Page or Marketplace listing, the same as the pages and **+ Create** hide them.
* **Search** ("Search people, chats, groups and the Marketplace") suggests, a few of each kind in turn, people (by
  name or @name, with how you are related), your chats (by name or their last message), public groups, pages,
  things for sale (not for a child) and, from Family, people in your household (choosing one opens a chat with
  them) and family events (the calendar opens with the event highlighted). A `#tag` offers its posts. **Enter**
  opens **Results for "…"** (`#/social?tab=search&q=…`): everyone and everything that matches, by kind, with
  **Add friend** or **Message** beside each person; with no match it offers **Find people** and **Search the
  Marketplace**.
* **Folders**: Social's sections (Home, Explore, Reels, Chats, Friends, Dating, Groups, Pages, Marketplace,
  Notifications, Saved, Profile and, for the household's owner, Moderation) and Family's tabs are folders in the left
  menu, each with its own icon, and the unread chats and notifications are counted on them. Only the open section's
  folders show. On a wide screen the folders stand in for the page's own tab bar; on a phone Social's bottom bar
  does (Home, Explore, Reels, Chats, Alerts, More), in place of the frame's own bar.

## Where the data lives

Social has one database for the whole server (`social.sqlite3`): next to the accounts database on this
computer, in the data folder of the hosted product. Uploaded photos are files in the media folder Videos uses
(`AIRA_MEDIA_DIR`, else `media` next to the databases or in the hosted data folder), under `social/`; the database
keeps who uploaded each, its type and size. The key that signs photo addresses lives in the Social database. People are identified by their household profile (this
computer) or their organization membership (hosted), never by email address.

Because the file sits outside every organization's database, the hosted product handles it explicitly: a
person's own export carries `social.json` (their profile, posts, comments, messages they wrote, stories,
listings and friends, never other people's words); removing a member erases their Social account and everything they wrote;
deleting an organization erases all of its members' accounts. Groups, pages and chats they made stay for
the other people in them. Tests point Social at a scratch file (`tests/conftest.py`), never the real one.

Dating's tables live in the same file (`so_dating_profiles`, `so_dating_swipes`, `so_dating_matches`,
`so_dating_daily`; `aira/suite/social/dating.py`), and a match's chat is a Social chat marked
`context = 'dating'`. `social.json` never carries Dating (not the profile, not the messages of a match's chat):
the person downloads it themselves from Dating. Erasing a Social account erases its Dating too.
