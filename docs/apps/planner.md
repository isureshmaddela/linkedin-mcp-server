# Planner

Run the home together: chores, meals, shopping, money and notes for everyone in the household, at any age, and
earn on the side by selling what you no longer need and by doing side jobs for neighbours, then save it toward
the things that matter.
Open it from the app switcher (Everyday > Planner), or from Today with the Home button and the Planner tile.

## My day

What you need to do today, with a big **Do it** button for each task, anything overdue, what is coming up this
week, today's meals, bills due soon (adults) and your shopping lists. Type into "Add something to do today" for a
quick task. Your chores also appear on **Today** with a **Done** button, and a task with a time pops up as a
reminder when the time comes (it can be read aloud in easy mode).

## Chores

* A task is for one person, **anyone** (whoever does it first) or **everyone** (each person ticks it for
  themselves, like "brush teeth").
* Give it a day and, if you like, a time for a reminder.
* Give it a time without a day and it is for today (a reminder needs a day).
* Repeats: every day, every weekday, every week on chosen days, every month on a day (the 31st becomes the
  last day of shorter months), or every few days. When a repeating task is done, the next one appears; doing it
  twice by mistake changes nothing, and **Not done** puts it back and takes its points away. A repeating task
  cannot be moved onto a day where the same task already is. A chore done late catches up: the next one is today
  or later, never more days already in the past.
* **Take turns** (adults): tick two or more people for a repeating chore ("Wash up: Mia, Leo"). Each time it is
  done, the next one goes to the next person, and each earns their own points. The chore says whose turn is
  next. Someone who leaves the household is skipped; when only one person is left, the chore is simply theirs
  (it still saves, whatever is changed). Choosing someone in **Who** who is not taking turns is explained, not
  quietly overridden. Children cannot change who takes turns.
* **Search** finds a task by its words or notes; adults can show one person's tasks; **Download CSV** saves the
  list for a spreadsheet.
* Adults see everyone's tasks. Children and teens see their own, "anyone" and "everyone" tasks, can add tasks for
  themselves, and cannot give themselves points. A child cannot un-tick a chore someone else did (that would take
  the other person's points away); the person who did it, or an adult, can.

## Meals

* A week at a glance, Monday to Sunday, with breakfast, lunch and dinner for each day. **Plan** a meal from a
  saved recipe or just write it ("Pizza night"), and say who is cooking. Planning a meal where one already is
  changes it rather than adding a second.
* **Recipes** keep the ingredients, one per line ("500 g minced beef", "2 onions": the amount is read from the
  start of the line), the minutes and how to make it.
* **Shop for this week's meals** puts the ingredients of the meals from today to Sunday on a shopping list (you
  choose which one if you have several). Something already waiting on the list is not added again, and an
  ingredient used by two meals goes on once. **Shop for it** does the same for one meal or recipe.
* **Copy last week** fills the empty meals of this week with last week's; planned meals stay.
* Everyone can see the plan and suggest meals (children too). Changing or removing a meal or recipe is for adults
  and for whoever added it. Today shows **Meals today**.

## Shopping

* Several lists (Groceries, Pharmacy...), which you can rename. Items are sorted by aisle automatically (fruit and
  vegetables, bakery, dairy, meat and fish, cupboard, frozen, drinks, household, pharmacy).
* Tap **Get** to tick an item; ticked items move to "In the basket" and **Clear ticked items** empties it.
* **Your usual items** are the things you buy most, one tap to add again.
* Lists update by themselves: two people shopping in different shops see each other's ticks within a few seconds.
* **Share this list** (adults): a link a relative can open without an account to see the list, tick items and add
  things, with their name. Turn the link off at any time.

## Budget (adults)

Money in and money spent per month, a monthly budget per category (tap a category to set it, or **Add a
category**), what is left or over, a chart of where the money went, **the last 6 months** (money in, spent and
left over each month) and a CSV download (money in is positive, money spent negative, so a spreadsheet's total is
what is left). Tap an entry to change it. Bills you pay, things you sell, side jobs you do and pocket money you
pay out land here by themselves; removing one of those entries undoes it (the bill is unpaid again, the thing is
for sale again, the job is taken off the record, the points come back). Children and teens cannot open it.

## Savings (adults)

**Save for something**: what it is, how much you need and, if you like, the day you need it by. Each goal shows
what is put aside (as words and a bar), what is still to go and, with a day, about how much to put aside each
month to get there on time (or that the day has passed). **Put money aside** and **Take some out** (never more
than is there); each line can be undone. Money from a thing you sell or a side job you do can go straight toward
a goal (choose it in **Sold** or **Job done**); undoing the sale or the job takes it back out. A goal never holds
less than nothing: when that money was taken out of the goal since, undoing the sale or job (or removing or
lowering its budget entry) is refused until it is put back or the taking out is undone. Putting money aside
is not spending, so the budget does not change. The tab adds up what is put aside, how much this month and how
much is needed each month for every goal.

## Bills (adults)

Monthly, yearly or one-off bills with the usual amount and due day, and what they come to each month. A bill added
after its day this month (or this year) is first due next time, not overdue straight away. **Paid** records the
payment once and adds it to the budget as spending; **Undo last payment** takes it back. Bills due in
the next three days show on Today with a **Paid** button, and a bill due today pops up as a reminder (not for bills
paid automatically).

## Sell (adults): earn from what you no longer need

* **Sell something**: what it is, a price (empty to give it away), its condition and a description.
* **Your sale page** is one link for everything you are selling. Neighbours and friends open it without an
  account, see what is for sale (and what is kept for someone) and tap **I want this** with their name, a phone
  number or email, an offer if they like and a message. They never see offers, other buyers or what something
  sold for. Give the page a title and a note ("Collection from the High Street, evenings").
* When someone asks, it shows on Today (**People want to buy**) and pops up as a reminder, with **Keep it for
  them**. Keeping it for a buyer stops new requests; **Put back on sale** if they do not come.
* **Sold** asks what it sold for (their offer or your price to start with) and adds it to the budget as money in
  (category Selling) and to the earnings Aira adds up across your apps. **Not sold after all** takes it back.
* A sale's or a job's money reads the same everywhere. Changing its budget entry (amount or day) changes the sale
  or job, Sell's and My day's "Earned", any savings goal and Aira's earnings line too; removing the entry undoes
  the sale or job. A sold thing taken off the list still counts as earned, and its entry still undoes it.
* The tab shows what you earned this month and in total, what is for sale and what is waiting for an answer;
  search, a filter and a CSV download. Aira does not take payments: you and the buyer settle in person.

## Side jobs (adults): earn by helping neighbours

* **Offer a side job**: babysitting, dog walking, ironing, gardening, tutoring... with a price for a job or for an
  hour (or empty to agree it each time). It is on the same sale page, under **Jobs we can do**.
* A neighbour taps **Ask about this** with their name, a phone number or email and a message (no offer box). It
  shows on Today (**People are asking**, with **Say yes**) and pops up as a reminder.
* **Say yes** books them; the job stays on offer for others. **Done and paid** (or **Record a job** for a regular
  customer) records what it earned: hours times the hourly price, or the price, or what you type. Each job is money
  in once (category Side jobs) and counts in the earnings Aira adds up across your apps; it can go straight toward
  a savings goal. **Undo** takes it back (the neighbour's request is booked again). A job recorded for an earlier
  day counts in that day's month everywhere, Aira's earnings included; a day that has not come yet is refused,
  and so are hours that are not a number.
* The Sell screen stays up to date while it is open: a new request, or **Say yes** in the pop-up alert, shows
  without moving away and back.
* On the public page a problem with the form (a contact that is not a phone number or email, a thing just kept
  for someone else) shows inside that form, on screen, with the field to fix in focus.
* **Pause** takes the job off the page and stops new requests; **Take jobs again** puts it back. The card shows
  how many jobs were done and what they earned, this month and in total.

## Rewards and pocket money

Adults set **points** on chores and add **rewards** ("Pick the film = 20 points"). Children earn points by doing
chores and tap **Ask for this**; an adult approves (the points are spent) or says not now. Everyone sees their
own points history; adults see everyone's points.

**Pocket money** (adults): set what 100 points are worth (for example 1.00), and **Pay out** turns a child's
points into money when you hand it over: the points are spent, the money is added to the budget as spending
(category Children), and the list shows what was paid. Points already promised to a reward cannot be paid out.
**Undo** gives the points back. Children see "Pocket money" in their points history and are told their points
can become pocket money, never amounts of money.

## In the Aira frame

Planner lives at its own address (`/planner`) with its logo in the top bar, like one app in a suite.

* **+ New** (above the left menu): **New task**, **Add to the shopping list** (the list's add box, ready to type),
  **Plan a meal**, **New note** and, for adults, **Sell something**. Each opens the same form as the button on its
  tab; Back or a refresh does not open it again.
* **Search** (the box across the top) finds chores, shopping lists and items, recipes (by name or ingredient),
  notes (shared ones and your own private ones) and rewards; adults also find bills, things for sale and side jobs,
  and savings goals. Choosing one opens its tab with it outlined (a chore or recipe search also fills that tab's
  own search box). **Enter** shows every match, kind by kind, under **Search results**.
* **The left menu** lists the tabs as folders, each with its icon: My day, Chores, Shopping, Meals, Budget,
  Savings, Bills, Sell (the money folders for adults only), Notes and Rewards. The page is named after the open
  folder. On a phone, where the menu is a sheet, the tab bar under the title stays.

## Privacy

Budget, savings, bills, selling, side jobs and pocket money are for adults only, checked on the server. Private notes are only their
writer's. Children see only their own chores and points. The public pages show only what a visitor needs: the
shopping list without who added what, and the sale page without offers or buyers. In the hosted build the
household is your organization's members, and another organization never sees your Planner.

(chores and taking turns, points, pocket money, shopping, budget and its six-month trend, bills, notes, the

requests, sold and unsold, jobs done, the public sale page), `goals.py` (savings goals, money in and out, money
from sales and jobs) and `planner_routes.py` (routes under `/api/planner/*`, Today cards, pulse, public pages). Routes added for the features: `GET/POST /meals`, `PATCH/DELETE /meals/{id}`, `POST /meals/copy`,
`POST /meals/shop`, `GET/POST /recipes`, `PATCH/DELETE /recipes/{id}`, `GET /sell` (`format=csv`),
`POST /sell/items`, `PATCH/DELETE /sell/items/{id}`, `POST /sell/items/{id}/sold|unsold|available`,
`POST /sell/requests/{id}/accept|decline`, `POST /sell/settings`, `POST/DELETE /sell/link`, `GET /pocket`,
`POST /pocket/rate`, `POST /pocket/{profile}/pay`, `DELETE /pocket/payouts/{id}`; `GET /tasks` takes `q`,
`person` and `format=csv`. Third round: tasks take `rotation` (a list of profile ids, adults); `POST /sell/items`
takes `kind` (`thing` or `service`) and `price_unit` (`job` or `hour`); `GET /sell?status=services`;
`POST /sell/items/{id}/pause`; `POST /sell/items/{id}/jobs` (`amount_cents`, `hours`, `customer`, `day`,
